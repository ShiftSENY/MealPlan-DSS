Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

MealPlan DSS is a personal weekly meal planning tool. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and the choices you have over it. We keep the description as short and plain as possible.

1. What we collect

We collect only what the app needs to work:

  • Account information: your name and email address, and a user identifier assigned by our sign-in provider (Clerk). Signing in is required to save data.
  • Your app data: the meal planning content you enter — ingredients and inventory, meal catalog entries, weekly plans, shopping lists, and preferences (budget, cooking time, dietary tags). Each record is keyed to your account.

We do not collect payment information, advertise, or use third-party analytics or tracking beacons. There is no behavioral profiling.

2. How we use it

Your data is used for one purpose: making the app work for you. That includes:

  • showing your saved inventory, meal catalog, plans and shopping lists across sessions and devices;
  • ranking meal recommendations against your inventory and preferences;
  • letting you back up and restore your own data.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or marketing partners.

3. Who processes the data

  • Clerk (clerk.dev) — handles authentication. Your smooth sign-in credentials are processed by Clerk, not by us.
  • Our hosting provider — the MySQL database that stores your app data is hosted on our cPanel account (jensentortega.online). Data is transmitted over HTTPS to the app.

On-premises or third parties receive data only when legally required, and then only after reasonable notice where possible.

4. Cookies and local storage

  • Essential authentication cookies (Clerk) — keep you signed in.
  • Service worker cache — the app's HTML/CSS/JS shell and static assets are cached so the PWA works offline. This cache contains no personal data.
  • localStorage (small app state) — things like recently used inventory categories, so the interface is faster on your next visit.

No analytics or marketing cookies are used. API responses from our server are never cached by the service worker.

5. Your rights

You can, at any time:

  • Access your data inside the app (every screen shows your own rows);
  • Export a full backup from your account menu (**Download backup (.json)**);
  • Update your preferences and delete any record from the app;
  • Request deletion of your account and all related app data by emailing us (see Section 7). Deletion may require manual cleanup because the underlying table structure backs up every user via `INSERT … ON DUPLICATE KEY UPDATE` idempotency.

6. Retention

App data is kept as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove your profile row and all rows scoped to your `user_id` (inventory, meal catalog, plans, shopping lists). Weekly plans created before account deletion are removed along with the account.

7. Contact

Privacy or data questions: jensent.ortega_cyn@isu.edu.ph.

MealPlan DSS is a learning/personal-use project. This policy describes the current deployment; it may be updated as the app changes.
